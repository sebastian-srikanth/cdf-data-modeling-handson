# participants/

Your module goes here, in a folder named after you:
`participants/<YOURNAME>/` — see
[Chapter 01](../../../docs/01-naming-isolation-and-setup.md) §1.4.

Everyone works in their own folder. That, plus the `(space, externalId)` identity
rule explained in Chapter 01 §1.2, is what lets a dozen people build the *same*
data model in the *same* CDF project without a single collision.

Participant configs (`training/config.<YOURNAME>-training.yaml`) are gitignored —
you author your own in Chapter 01 §1.5.
